Running Information

Everything you need to know before exploring the beautiful running trails around Bussum.

Running in Nature

The natural areas around Bussum and Hilversum offer beautiful places to run. Enjoy the forests, heathlands and peaceful surroundings while respecting the environment.

Trail Etiquette

Stay on marked paths, give other visitors space and avoid disturbing wildlife. Take your rubbish home and help keep nature clean.

Safety

Choose a route that matches your fitness level. Bring water, check the weather and tell someone where you are going.

Accessibility

Some routes have sandy or uneven surfaces. Check the route details before visiting if you need a more accessible path.

Respect the Environment

Running outdoors is a great way to enjoy nature. Small actions help protect these beautiful areas for everyone.

Protect Wildlife

Keep a respectful distance from animals and avoid making unnecessary noise.

Stay on the Trails

Follow marked routes to protect plants and sensitive natural areas.

Share the Paths

Be considerate of walkers, cyclists and other runners.
