Essential Gear

You don't need much to get started, but the right gear can make your run more comfortable and enjoyable.

Running Shoes

Choose comfortable, well-fitting shoes with good grip. The trails around Bussum and Hilversum can be uneven and sometimes muddy.

Clothing

Wear breathable, moisture-wicking clothes. Dress in layers, as the weather in the Netherlands can change quickly.

Hydration

Bring water, especially for longer runs. A small backpack or waist belt can be useful.

Watch / GPS

Use a GPS watch or running app to track your route, pace and distance. It also helps you stay on the right path.

Before You Go

A quick checklist to make sure you're ready.

Check the weather

Be prepared for rain, wind or cold conditions. Conditions can change quickly in the Netherlands.

Fuel your body

Eat something light 1-2 hours before your run. For longer runs, bring energy snacks.

Plan your route

Know the distance and terrain. Use a map or GPS app.

Stay safe

Let someone know your route and estimated time.

Warm up

Do a light warm-up and dynamic stretches to prevent injuries.

Be mindful

Respect nature, other trail users and local rules.

Weather and Trail Conditions

The weather and terrain can change throughout the year. Here's what to keep in mind:

Spring & Summer

Mild temperatures, longer days. Bring water and sun protection.

Autumn

Cooler temperatures, beautiful colours. Trails can be wet and slippery.

Winter

Cold and possibly icy conditions. Wear warm layers and shoes with good grip.

Terrain

Expect a mix of paved paths, gravel, sand and forest trails. Some areas can be muddy or uneven.
